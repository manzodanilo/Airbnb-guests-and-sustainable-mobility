# Airbnb guests and sustainable mobility

Excel analysis of 300 Airbnb stays, exploring who the guests are, how much they spend and which vehicles they rent during their stay, with a focus on low-impact mobility.

Project completed during the *Data Science, Analytics and AI Agents* program at start2impact.

## What I did

- **Data cleaning:** checked for duplicate guest IDs and fixed a misspelled country name.
- **Calculated fields:** total spend, exact age and 0/1 flags for each type of vehicle rental.
- **Analysis:** answered 15 business questions on guests, spending and vehicle rentals, using dynamic array functions (`UNIQUE`, `FILTER`, `SORT`) and pivot tables.

## Key findings

- **289 unique guests** out of 300 bookings, after removing duplicates.
- In cities rated 6 or more for environmental care, guests rented **117 low-impact vehicles** (60 bicycles and 57 scooters) **versus 67 cars**.
- Average spend of **about €330 per family member**.

## Tools

Excel · UNIQUE, FILTER, SORT · Pivot tables · Data cleaning
